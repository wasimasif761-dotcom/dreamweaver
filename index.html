About Relax & Focus

Relax & Focus (Pure Noise) is a minimalist ambient sound generator designed to help you concentrate, relax, study, and fall asleep peacefully.

Everything runs 100% locally in your browser using the Web Audio API with zero network latency and full offline support.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is 100% protected. Relax & Focus (Pure Noise) does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All sound synthesis runs purely client-side on your device.

Terms of Use
